# Stylized Asset Family Production

Start from the approved art bible and a family sheet (silhouettes, palette, textures/material density, lighting, scale, camera distance and performance budget). Show or cite 2–3 legitimate conceptual directions; obtain human approval for major visual work. Do not begin a final map using unrelated free assets or call a geometric blockout the final asset pass.

1. Search approved internal assets first. For a coherent no-cost low-poly/stylized family, evaluate **Quaternius** CC0 model packs and **Kenney** CC0 individual packs; for materials/HDRIs consider **Poly Haven** CC0 only if its realism fits the art bible. Roblox Creator Store may provide useful game-native meshes but inspect bundled scripts/permissions and verify applicable rights. Openverse is discovery, not asset-license certification. Record original pages, actual licenses, hashes, import rights and attribution where required.
2. Build or adapt **family members**, not disconnected one-offs: normalize scale, silhouette language, shared atlas/materials, pivots, collisions, geometry quality, rig requirements and textures using approved Roblox tooling or local Blender when installed and safely configured. Generate primitives only for blockouts or a specifically approved geometric final aesthetic.
3. Import to a dedicated, backed-up Studio development place. World assets stay Studio-owned unless a reviewed Rojo/asset source mapping explicitly versions them. Maintain source binaries and manifest with IDs/provenance; review any large binaries/LFS before Git inclusion.
4. Capture real in-game front/side/gameplay-camera screenshots with lighting and dressing, inspect density/silhouette and visual cohesion alongside UI. An independent visual reviewer and human creative owner approve substantive art. Stop with `ART_CAPABILITY_BLOCKED` if zero-cost/current tools cannot meet requirements; never silently relabel a brick proxy as a finished mesh.

A skill procedure improves selection and consistency; it does not itself provide access to a bespoke high-quality 3D generator. Local AI model generation may need substantial GPU power and separate license/security review; no free hosted generator is assumed.
